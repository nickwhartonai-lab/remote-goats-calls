The big idea

You own your list.
You rent your followers.

📧

Your email list: yours

Real addresses in your account. You hit send, it lands in the inbox. No algorithm deciding who hears from you.

🔒 You control it
📱

Your followers: borrowed

They live on Instagram's land. Instagram decides reach, changes the rules, and can shut it down. Nothing you can do.

⚠️ They control it

If Instagram disappeared tonight, your email list would still be yours tomorrow. The other way around is not true.

Not being dramatic

This happens to real people

🔓

Hacked

Accounts with tens of thousands of followers gone in a couple of hours.

🚫

Suspended

Banned overnight for something they didn't do, with almost no way to appeal.

🧹

Purged

Platform-wide cleanups wipe millions of followers in a single day.

No email list? You start from zero. With a list, you log in, send one message, and you're back.

The definition

So what's a lead magnet?

Nobody wakes up wanting more email. You give them a reason. That reason is your lead magnet.

⇄
📧

Permission to land in their inbox

They get a quick win. You get permission to show up in their inbox.

How to pick yours

The 3 rules of a good lead magnet

1

Solves one problem, fast

Not "everything about investing." One thing they're stuck on that you can solve in about 30 minutes.

2

Points to what you'll sell

The first small step toward your future course or service. Right freebie, right people.

3

A quick win up front

Consumable in about an hour, with a fast win at the start. A long ebook with no quick win just sits in their downloads, unopened.

One problem. One promise. One quick win.

Easiest = best

The best formats for beginners

✅ Checklist 📝 Template 📄 Cheat sheet 📘 Short ebook 🧰 Resource list

The easiest ones to make are also the ones that work best. A checklist is just a Google Doc you save as a PDF in twenty minutes.

What kills it

The mistakes to avoid

  • ✕
    Too broad. "A free guide to getting healthy." Speaks to no one.
  • ✕
    Too long. Takes an afternoon, so they never get the win.
  • ✕
    Not tied to anything. A random freebie. You collect the wrong emails.
  • ✕
    "Sign up for my newsletter." That's not a lead magnet. It offers nothing in return.

The title sells it

Name the outcome, not the topic

Topic (boring)

"Content marketing guide"

→
Outcome (wanted)

"30-Day Content Calendar"

"The [number]-step checklist to [result]" · "How to [result] in [timeframe] without [the annoying part]" · "The exact template I use to [result]"

Put a number in it. Name a clear result. Keep it believable. Seven days, not overnight.
